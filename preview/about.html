Clean, affordable rides for every Bangladeshi.

Ezygo rents electric scooters and e-bikes to riders, delivery partners and businesses. We want every short trip in Bangladesh to be zero-emission.

What we're working towards

Mission

Replace fossil-fuel vehicles with affordable, reliable electric ones, cutting air pollution in our cities and creating sustainable jobs.

Vision

A Bangladesh where every last-mile journey is zero-emission, on locally charged, locally operated electric vehicles.

Values

Sustainability, inclusion and innovation. Clean transport should be a right, not a luxury.

Md Sazzatul Islam

Founder and CEO

An electrical engineer and entrepreneur who founded Ezygo to make urban transport in Bangladesh sustainable through electric vehicles.

Under his leadership Ezygo has pioneered 72V lithium-powered solutions for urban mobility, built sustainable rental frameworks, and was named Most Impactful Business at the Renewable Energy Fest. His expertise covers lithium-ion batteries, EV logistics and sustainable infrastructure.
