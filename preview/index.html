No hidden charges. Our team verifies your documents when you pick up the bike.

  2. 2

    Bring your documents

    • National ID card (NID) or a valid passport
    • Two passport-size photos

Questions

How do I rent an Ezygo bike?

Send the booking form above with the bike and plan you want. Our team confirms within 2 hours, then you bring your documents and pick up the bike.

What documents do I need?

Your NID or a valid passport, two passport-size photos, a parent's NID, a recent electricity or water bill, and an active mobile number.

How much does it cost?

The EM1 costs Tk 299 a day. Weekly and monthly plans are available, and for Sparky and Eco Ride prices, call or message us.

Is the bike insured?

Yes. Third-party insurance is included on every rental plan, and our support team is available 24/7 for roadside help.

Can my business get a fleet?

Yes. We offer delivery fleets and full white-label packages with your branding. See our business services or get in touch below.
