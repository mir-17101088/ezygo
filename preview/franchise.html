How it works

FOCO

  1. Step 1

    Invest in a hub

    Secure a 200–400 sq ft location and fund the setup: scooters, charging and branding.

  2. Step 2

    We take over operations

    Our team runs rentals, maintenance, battery swaps and customer relations.

  3. Step 3

    Earn every month

    You receive a revenue share each month, with digital reports.

  4. Step 4

    Scale when ready

    Add scooters or open more hubs in new areas.

FOFO

  1. Step 1

    Set up your hub

    We help you find a location and set up charging, branding and the fleet.

  2. Step 2

    Train with Ezygo

    Two weeks of training on fleet operations, the app, customers and batteries.

  3. Step 3

    Run it yourself

    You manage rentals, maintenance and customer service, with our technology and support.

  4. Step 4

    Keep higher margins

    No operations fee. You pay a brand royalty only.
