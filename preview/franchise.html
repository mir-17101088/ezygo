How FOFO Works

  1. 1

    Set Up Your Hub

    We help you find the right location, set up charging, branding, and fleet. Full turnkey support.

  2. 2

  3. 3

    Operate Independently

    You manage daily rentals, maintenance, and customer service — with our tech and support backbone.

    No ops fee means more revenue stays with you. Pay only a brand royalty percentage.
